How to file your complaint

You've got your due process complaint drafted. Here's how to file it in New York — it's a few plain steps.

  1. Download the official NYSED cover form

    It's already set up so you only fill in basic information — the "Nature of Complaint" and "Proposed Resolution" boxes already say "See attached."

    Download the NYSED complaint form (PDF)
  2. Fill in your basic information

    Your name, your child's name, your address, and your school district. That's all the form itself needs from you.

  3. Attach the complaint letter Plainly drafted

    That letter is the "See attached" substance — it's the actual nature of your complaint and what you're asking for. Attach it to the cover form as one package.

  4. Mail it to two places

    Send the original to your school district, and send one copy to NYSED:

    New York State Education Department
    Office of Special Education
    Room 309 EB, 89 Washington Avenue
    Albany, NY 12234
    Attn: Due Process Unit

    Send it certified mail so you have proof of delivery, and keep a copy for yourself.

These steps are for filing a due process complaint. Your school district's own mailing address varies — Plainly can help you find it in the chat. Plainly is a conversational guide, not a lawyer, and nothing here is legal advice.
